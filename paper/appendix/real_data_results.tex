\section{Full Real-Data Benchmark Results}\label{app:real-data-results}

This appendix contains the full real-data benchmark tables supporting Section~\ref{subsec:exp_real}. The main text reports the headline plots and selected summaries; here we keep the aggregate tables, per-dataset tables, BDF selection diagnostics, and compact rank diagrams needed for auditability.

\subsection{Regression benchmark details}\label{app:regression-additional}

Tables~\ref{tab:rel-to-best-crps} and~\ref{tab:rankings-crpss} give the aggregate relative-CRPS and CRPSS-rank summaries quoted in Section~\ref{subsubsec:exp_regression}, Table~\ref{tab:win-tie-loss} the per-dataset sign-test counts, and Table~\ref{tab:crps-per-dataset} the underlying per-dataset CRPS values with fold standard errors. The NGBoost entries reflect the retained catastrophic Exponential fit on \texttt{forest\_fires} discussed in Appendix~\ref{app:experimental-setup-reg-models}. Figure~\ref{fig:pit-histograms} reports the per-method PIT histograms behind the calibration claims, and Figure~\ref{fig:rmse-rel-to-best} the relative-RMSE summary used in the point-accuracy comparison.
